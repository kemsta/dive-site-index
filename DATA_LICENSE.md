# Using and licensing the data

## Short answer

Yes. The Dive Site Index database may be used in free or commercial products under the [Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0 (ODC-By 1.0)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/).

ODC-By permits sharing, modification, integration, and commercial use. It requires attribution whenever the database, a substantial part of it, a derivative database, or a work produced from it is used publicly within the scope of the license.

Unlike ODbL, ODC-By does **not** impose the ODbL share-alike obligation to make a derivative database available merely because a product made from it is used publicly. If you publicly convey a copy of this database or a derivative database, ODC-By's license and notice requirements still apply.

## Required attribution

A suitable notice is:

> Contains information from [Dive Site Index](https://kemsta.github.io/dive-site-index/), made available under the [Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/).

Place the notice somewhere users can reasonably find it—for example in an About, Credits, Data sources, or Legal screen. When distributing the database or a derivative database, retain its license and notices as required by ODC-By 1.0.

## Alternative terms by individual agreement

The database rightsholder reserves the right to offer separate terms, including permission to publish without displaying the standard ODC-By license notice, under an individual written agreement. Any attribution requirements for that use are determined by the agreement.

An alternative agreement applies only to the parties, data, uses, and rights it expressly identifies. It does not withdraw or restrict ODC-By 1.0 rights already granted to other users, and it cannot grant rights in third-party material that the project does not own or control.

To request alternative terms, open a licensing request in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/kemsta/dive-site-index/issues) without including confidential terms; a private contact channel can then be established if needed.

## Scope

ODC-By 1.0 covers the **Dive Site Index database as a database**: its selection, arrangement, catalog records, and published database exports to the extent the project controls the applicable rights.

It does not grant rights the project does not own or control, including rights in third-party source material. Source references identify supporting sources; they are not relicensed by this project. Do not assume that linked text, images, trademarks, or other third-party material is covered by ODC-By.
